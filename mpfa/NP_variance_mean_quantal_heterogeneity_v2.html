Each repetition draws a binomial number of released quanta (k ~ Bin(N, p)), sums k quantal fluorescence signals with Q scatter, and adds Gaussian fluorescence noise. The right panel overlays the analytic mixture: the peak at 0 is release failures, and higher peaks are 1, 2, ... released quanta. With Train mode on, the protocol is the full train and the histogram uses the selected stimulus number.
